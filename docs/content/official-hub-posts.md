## #rules

### DeCave Community Rules

These rules apply everywhere on DeCave: hubs, DMs, group chats, voice and Find a Squad.

1. **Be respectful.** No harassment, bullying, threats or targeted insults.
2. **No hate speech.** Nothing that attacks people based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or nationality.
3. **Keep it safe for work in public spaces.** No sexual, gory or shocking content in hubs that haven't opted into it. Never anything involving minors.
4. **Protect privacy.** Don't share anyone's personal information (real name, address, photos, accounts) without their consent.
5. **No cheating, scams or exploits.** No selling cheats, account trading, phishing links, fake giveaways or impersonating DeCave staff.
6. **No spam.** No mass DMs, repeated messages, unsolicited advertising or self-promotion outside spaces that allow it.
7. **Follow each hub's own rules.** Hub owners can add stricter rules for their community.
8. **Respect the law** and the [Terms of Service](https://de-cave.com/terms).

**Enforcement:** depending on severity, breaking these rules can lead to a warning, temporary restrictions, removal from a hub, or a permanent ban from DeCave.

**Report a problem:** right-click a message or profile and choose **Report**. Reports are private and the person is never told who reported them.

## #faq

### Is this hub private? Can other people see me here?
Yes, it's private. Everyone joins DeCave Official automatically, but members can't see each other: the member list, member counts and online status are hidden, and only the DeCave team can post. Nobody can tell you're here.

### How do I set up my profile?
Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner → **Profile**. You can add a picture, banner, bio and the games you play.

### How do I add friends?
Open **Friends**, choose **Add Friend** and search by username. Once they accept, you can chat, call and invite them to hubs.

### What is Find a Squad?
Pick a game and what you're looking for, and DeCave matches you with players looking for the same thing. When you match, you get a squad room to chat and voice together.

### How do I join or create a hub?
Use the **+** button in the top bar. You can browse public hubs, join one with an invite link, or create your own from a template (including the Streamer layout).

### How do voice rooms work?
Click a voice room in any hub to join. You can mute, deafen, share your screen and change your input device from the voice controls. Push-to-talk is available in the desktop app.

### Should I use the desktop app?
It's optional, but it adds game detection, desktop notifications, global push-to-talk and auto-updates. Download it for Windows or Mac at [de-cave.com](https://de-cave.com).

### How do I report someone?
Right-click their message or profile and choose **Report**. Reports are private.

### Where can I see what's new?
Read **#announcements** for news and **#patch-notes** for every update.

### How do I delete my account or my data?
Go to **Settings → Account**. See the [Privacy Policy](https://de-cave.com/privacy) for details on what's stored and how it's removed.
